Data sources: CricketData.org (fixtures, match IDs) and Cricbuzz on RapidAPI (squads, recent scorecards, pitch notes) are the two keyed cricket APIs; Cricsheet and Open-Meteo are open data. Predicted XI: continuity 30 · form 30 · record 20 · against this opponent 10 · freshness 10, then balance rules (one keeper, at least five bowling options, spin and pace). AI: Gemini Flash only; it works from the computed numbers and searched news and never invents statistics. Search falls back to OpenRouter (Google models only) when the direct allowance runs out. Hosting: Vercel (static site + serverless functions), Neon for cache and history.
